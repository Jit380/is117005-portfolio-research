## Key Research Findings

1. **Internships emphasize foundations.** Programming, problem-solving, communication, and project experience appear across multiple postings.
2. **AI roles vary by specialization.** Addepar focuses on applied AI, National Grid on ML infrastructure, and Gecko Robotics on production machine learning systems.
3. **Experienced FDE roles require customer-facing expertise.** Teleskope expects professional experience with deployment and enterprise technical support.
4. **Eligibility matters.** Graduation dates, work authorization, location, and internship schedules differ by employer.
5. **Research limitations.** This sample is concentrated in New York. A verified New Jersey-specific AI posting should be added during the next research update.

## Research Method

- Used original employer career pages and applicant-tracking systems.
- Recorded exact job titles, locations, technical responsibilities, eligibility constraints, and source URLs.
- Distinguished internships from experienced full-time roles.
- Did not treat an accessible job description as a guarantee that applications remain open.
- Posting details should be rechecked before applying.

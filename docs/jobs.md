# NY / NJ job research tracker

## Verification in progress

This section is reserved for real employer postings verified through Chrome after the documentation site is set up. No example listing should be mistaken for an actual vacancy.

The research will cover internships, entry-level, mid-level, and senior roles across forward deployment, security, systems/IT, infrastructure, and operations. Exact titles, job URLs, locations, experience requirements, AI responsibilities, and dates checked will be recorded here.

## Inclusion rules

- Link directly to an employer or its applicant-tracking system.
- Confirm a New York or New Jersey location; distinguish remote eligibility from an actual local office role.
- Include a substantive AI responsibility rather than incidental use of an AI tool.
- Preserve the employer's exact title and stated qualifications.
- Mark seniority inferred from requirements when no level is explicit.
- Record unavailable, closed, or inaccessible postings honestly.

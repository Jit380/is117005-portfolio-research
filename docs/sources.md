# Sources & methodology

Research date: **November 5, 2026**. The site separates occupation statistics, employer expectations, real job advertisements, and original analysis.

| Source | Used for | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [BLS: Data scientists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm) | U.S. occupation description and 2024–2034 growth projection | Broad occupation; not an AI-only or NY/NJ estimate. |
| [BLS: AI, IT, and employment](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/artificial-intelligence-information-technology-and-employment-2024-34.htm) | National software developer and technology employment context | Projections are not current vacancy counts. |
| [WEF: Future of Jobs 2025, jobs outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook) | Global employer expectations through 2030 | Different geography, period, and method from BLS. |
| [Anthropic employer careers](https://www.anthropic.com/careers/jobs) | Examples of AI specialization titles | One employer; postings change and may close. |
| [NIST: AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) | Context for governance, risk, and evaluation work | Not employment-growth evidence. |

## Posting verification protocol

Search broadly in Chrome, then open the employer's original advertisement. Capture exact title, employer, location, work arrangement, stated level, required experience, AI-specific duties, direct URL, and date checked. Label salary only when disclosed by the employer and preserve the location and pay-period context.

Distinguish a verified open advertisement from a search-result snippet or a closed page. When the level is not explicit, record the requirements and explain the classification. Do not infer local eligibility from “remote” alone.

## Research integrity

Descriptions on this site are original summaries. Direct links allow readers to inspect the underlying evidence. Suggested projects and interpretation are identified separately from employer requirements. No applications are submitted as part of this research.

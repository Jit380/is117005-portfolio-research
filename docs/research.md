# Research question & findings

<div class="research-note">IS 117005 · Research brief · November 5, 2026</div>

## Central question

Which emerging AI job titles offer credible career options in New York and New Jersey, what do they involve, and how do expectations change from internship to senior level?

## Initial findings

1. **The broader employment signal is strong.** BLS projects 34% growth for U.S. data scientists during 2024–2034. This supports investigating data and modeling careers, but does not establish growth for a particular title such as LLM engineer. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm)
2. **AI roles belong to a larger technology market.** Software developers have projected national employment growth of 15.8% during 2024–2034. AI application work is one specialization within that occupation. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/artificial-intelligence-information-technology-and-employment-2024-34.htm)
3. **Employer expectations also point toward AI.** The World Economic Forum places AI and machine learning specialists among the fastest-growing roles by percentage through 2030. Its global employer survey is a different measure from BLS projections. [WEF](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook)
4. **New titles need separate verification.** A live employer advertisement establishes that a job exists; it does not prove that the entire occupation is growing. The job tracker records individual openings and labels career-stage interpretations.

## Scope

- Geography: New York and New Jersey, prioritizing NYC and northern/central New Jersey. Remote U.S. roles must explicitly permit work from the region to qualify as local-compatible.
- Functions: engineering, forward deployment, cybersecurity, IT systems, infrastructure, operations, product, evaluation, and governance.
- Levels: internship, entry-level, mid-level, and senior.
- Evidence: employer/ATS advertisements for actual openings; BLS for occupation context; primary reports for broader trends.

## Questions for each posting

What is the exact title? What makes the work AI-specific? What does the person deliver? What experience and education are required? Where is the work performed? Is the advertised role genuinely accessible to a student or new graduate?

## Limits

This is a dated research snapshot, not an exhaustive census or a hiring guarantee. Listings expire. Job titles vary between employers. National growth and salaries cannot be treated as local forecasts or offer terms. Seniority is taken from the posting when possible and otherwise identified as an inference.
